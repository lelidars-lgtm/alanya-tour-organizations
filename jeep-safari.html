About This Experience

Jeep Safari in the Taurus Mountains is an active adventure that combines adrenaline, nature and the authentic atmosphere of Türkiye.

During the tour you will travel through mountain roads in comfortable off-road jeeps, climb to panoramic viewpoints with breathtaking landscapes, swim in cool mountain rivers and visit picturesque waterfalls.

Along the route you will discover traditional Turkish villages, fruit plantations and untouched mountain nature. During the stop you will enjoy a traditional Turkish lunch and experience the local culture away from crowded tourist areas.

This excursion is perfect for guests who want to discover the real side of Alanya — wild nature, mountain life and unforgettable adventure.
