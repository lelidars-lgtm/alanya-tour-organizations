About this experience

Jeep Safari in the Taurus Mountains is an active adventure combining off-road driving, nature and the authentic atmosphere of Turkey.

Travel along mountain roads, stop at panoramic viewpoints and enjoy refreshing breaks near a river and waterfall.

Along the route, discover traditional villages and fruit plantations, then relax over a traditional lunch included in the program.

It is an ideal choice for guests who want to see a more natural and adventurous side of Alanya.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hotel transfer included?

Yes. Pickup from your hotel or apartment and return transfer are included.

Is lunch included?

Yes. A traditional lunch is included. Drinks are paid separately.

Who drives the jeep?

A client may drive when they have a valid driving licence. If they do not have one, they can ride in the back with a family member, friend or companion who is licensed to drive.

Will there be a water fight?

Yes. A fun water battle is included, so bring a dry change of clothes and protect your phone.

Is the cave included?

No. The cave entrance is paid separately and costs $3.

What should I bring?

Bring sunglasses, a bandana or secure cap, sunscreen, SPF lip balm, comfortable footwear, a dry change of clothes, water and cash.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included in the tour price.

Should I book in advance?

Yes. Advance booking is recommended, especially in the high season.
