About this experience

Skydiving Experience — The Ultimate Adrenaline Rush in Turkey.

Experience one of the most unforgettable adventures of your life with a tandem skydive above the breathtaking Mediterranean coastline of Manavgat and Side.

Your journey begins with a scenic flight to an altitude of approximately 3,500 meters, offering spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Taurus Mountains, river delta landscapes and the golden beaches of the Turkish Riviera.

Then comes the moment you will never forget — an exhilarating freefall at speeds of up to 200 km/h. After the parachute opens, you will enjoy a peaceful canopy flight with incredible panoramic views before a smooth and controlled landing.

Dare to fly. Dare to experience true freedom.

Tour Program

1. Hotel Pick-up & Transfer

Your adventure begins with a comfortable pick-up from your hotel or residence. Fully insured, air-conditioned transfer and professional drivers are provided.

2. Arrival & Registration

Upon arrival at the Skydive Center, our team guides you through check-in, safety documentation, waiver forms, weight and health verification.

3. Meet Your Instructor

You will meet your certified tandem skydiving instructor and receive individually adjusted international-standard tandem equipment.

4. Safety Briefing

A clear 10–15 minute briefing explains jump procedures, freefall body position, canopy flight and landing technique.

5. Aircraft Boarding & Ascent

Together with your instructor, you board the aircraft and begin the scenic climb to jump altitude with final equipment and connection checks.

6. Tandem Skydive

Experience an unforgettable freefall from thousands of meters above the Turkish Riviera, followed by a safe parachute flight and controlled descent.

7. Landing & Celebration

After a smooth instructor-controlled landing, celebrate one of the most exciting moments of your life before your return transfer.
