About this experience

Skydiving Experience — The Ultimate Adrenaline Rush in Turkey.

Experience one of the most unforgettable adventures of your life with a tandem skydive above the breathtaking Mediterranean coastline of Manavgat and Side.

Your journey begins with a scenic flight to an altitude of approximately 10,000 feet (about 3,000–3,600 meters), offering spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Taurus Mountains, river delta landscapes and the golden beaches of the Turkish Riviera.

Then comes the moment you will never forget — an exhilarating freefall at speeds of up to 200 km/h. After the parachute opens, you will enjoy a peaceful canopy flight with incredible panoramic views before a smooth and controlled landing.

Tour Program

The sequence remains the same, while exact timing may change because of weather, aviation conditions, passenger preparation and the operator’s flight schedule.

Pickup

Hotel or Apartment Transfer

Pickup from your accommodation at the confirmed time and transfer to the Manavgat dropzone.

Check-in

Registration and Requirements Check

Identity, age, weight and health requirements are checked before participation documents are completed.

Briefing

Instructor and Safety Preparation

Meet your tandem instructor, receive professional equipment and learn the correct body position for exit, freefall and landing.

Ascent

Aircraft Boarding and Climb

Board the aircraft and climb for approximately 15–20 minutes toward the planned jump altitude.

Freefall

Tandem Skydive

Jump from approximately 10,000 feet and experience around 40–45 seconds of freefall at speeds of up to 200 km/h.

Canopy

Parachute Flight and Landing

Glide above the Mediterranean landscape for approximately 5–7 minutes before a controlled landing with your instructor.

Landing

Landing and Celebration

Meet the ground team, remove the equipment and celebrate the experience after a controlled instructor-led landing.

Return

Return Transfer

After the operation and optional media arrangements, return to your hotel or apartment.

Important Skydive Safety Rules

Participation is subject to the operator’s final safety approval. The instructor’s briefing, weather conditions, aviation requirements and equipment checks always take priority.

Instructor Instructions

Guests must attend the full safety briefing and follow all instructor and ground-team instructions during preparation, aircraft boarding, freefall, canopy flight and landing.

Age, Weight & Health

Minimum age is 16 with the required consent for minors. Maximum published weight is 100 kg. Health information, body proportions and safe harness fit are checked before approval.

Clothing & Personal Items

Wear closed sports shoes and comfortable clothing. Phones, GoPro cameras, loose jewellery, scarves and unsecured personal items are not permitted during the jump.

Weather & Operations

The operator may delay, change, reschedule or cancel the jump when wind, rain, visibility, air traffic or other operational conditions do not allow safe operation.

Safety has priority over the advertised schedule. The operator’s dropzone rules, instructor briefing, equipment requirements and aviation decisions always take priority.
