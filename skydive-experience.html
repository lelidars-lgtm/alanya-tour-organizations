About This Experience

SKYDIVING EXPERIENCE — THE ULTIMATE ADRENALINE RUSH IN TURKEY

Experience one of the most unforgettable adventures of your life with a tandem skydive above the breathtaking Mediterranean coastline of Manavgat and Side.

Your journey begins with a scenic flight to an altitude of approximately 3,500 meters, offering spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea, the Taurus Mountains, river delta landscapes and the golden beaches of the Turkish Riviera.

Then comes the moment you will never forget — an exhilarating freefall at speeds of up to 200 km/h. After the parachute opens, you will enjoy a peaceful canopy flight with incredible panoramic views before a smooth and controlled landing.

Whether you are seeking the ultimate adrenaline rush or simply want to experience Turkey from a completely different perspective, this skydive offers memories that will stay with you for a lifetime.

Dare to fly. Dare to experience true freedom.

Tour Program

Hotel Pick-up & Transfer

Your adventure begins with a comfortable pick-up from your hotel or residence. Fully insured, air-conditioned transfer and return transfer are included. Total experience duration is approximately 4 hours.

Arrival & Registration

Upon arrival at the Skydive Center, the team guides you through check-in, safety documentation, waiver forms and basic health, weight and height verification.

Meet Your Instructor

You will meet your certified tandem skydiving instructor, receive personalized equipment fitting and complete individual safety checks before the flight.

Safety Briefing

A clear pre-jump briefing explains jump procedures, freefall body position, canopy flight instructions, landing techniques and safety guidelines.

Aircraft Boarding & Ascent

Together with your instructor, you board the aircraft and begin the scenic climb to jump altitude with final equipment and tandem connection checks.

Tandem Skydive

Experience an adrenaline-filled freefall above the Mediterranean coastline, followed by a safe parachute flight and controlled descent.

Landing & Celebration

After a smooth instructor-controlled landing, celebrate one of the most exciting moments of your life. Optional professional photo and video packages are available.
