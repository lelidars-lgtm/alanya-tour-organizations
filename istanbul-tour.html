About this experience

Istanbul by Plane is a premium full-day journey to one of the most fascinating cities in the world — the place where East meets Europe and ancient empires left their timeless legacy.

This exclusive tour is created for guests who want to see the main symbols of the former Constantinople in one day, enjoy the atmosphere of the Bosphorus and feel the energy of Istanbul without wasting time on a long road transfer.

You will travel by plane and explore the city with a Russian-speaking guide. The program combines history, culture, panoramic viewpoints, gastronomy, iconic landmarks and the unforgettable rhythm of Istanbul.

A special highlight of the day is the Bosphorus Cruise, where you can admire Ottoman palaces, historic waterfront mansions, legendary bridges between Europe and Asia and one of the most beautiful city panoramas in the world.

For guests who prefer privacy and maximum comfort, a VIP Istanbul version can be arranged with private transfer, personal guide, premium viewpoints, private Bosphorus boat cruise, luxury restaurants and optional photo session.
