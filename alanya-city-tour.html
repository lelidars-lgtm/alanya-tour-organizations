About this experience

Alanya City Tour is a carefully designed sunset route that combines the city’s most famous coastline, natural landmark, ancient fortress, Ottoman architecture and panoramic viewpoints.

The tour begins at the world-famous Cleopatra Beach, known for its golden sand and clear Mediterranean water. Guests may also visit Damlataş Cave, a legendary natural cave with impressive stalactites and a unique microclimate.

From Cleopatra Beach, you will take the cable car up to Alanya Castle. Inside the castle area, the route continues through ancient fortress walls, Sultan Suleyman Mosque and the atmospheric Old Town streets of Alanya Kale.

The highlight of the evening is the sunset view over the Mediterranean Sea. After returning by cable car, the tour continues by vehicle to the I LOVE ALANYA viewpoint, passing banana plantations and local neighborhoods along the way.
