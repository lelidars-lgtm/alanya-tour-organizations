About this experience

Sealanya Dolphinpark is one of the largest and most famous dolphin parks on the Alanya coast.

Guests enjoy a spectacular show with dolphins, sea lions and seals demonstrating intelligence, grace and playful performance skills.

For the best viewing experience, we recommend choosing central seats. They offer the best overview of the show and help avoid splashes during the most impressive dolphin jumps.

Swimming with dolphins is available for an additional fee with advance reservation.
