About this experience

Sealanya Dolphinpark is located in Türkler, on the Alanya coast, and is presented by Sealanya as Türkiye’s largest marine mammal show centre.

The approximately 45–55 minute program begins with a seal performance and continues with the dolphin show, including coordinated movements, jumps and trainer interaction.

Officially, shows are scheduled every day at 10:30 and 15:00. The departure offered with your booking is confirmed according to transfer availability and the selected date.

Swimming with dolphins is a separate activity and is not included in the standard show ticket. It requires advance reservation and has additional age, height, swimming ability, pregnancy and health restrictions.

Professional photographs with dolphins or seals are also paid separately. Personal photography is not permitted during the dolphin swimming session.

The restrictions for dolphin swimming do not apply to ordinary show attendance. Show times, prices and activities may be changed by Sealanya. Final pickup and program details are confirmed after booking.

Important Dolphinpark Rules

Show Attendance

Guests must not feed or touch the animals unless instructed and supervised by trainers.

Smoking is prohibited in the amphitheatre and near the pools.

Swimming Requirements

Participants must know how to swim. A life jacket is compulsory, and the pool is approximately 6 metres deep.

Children aged 0–5 are not permitted. Children aged 6–7 may participate only with a paying adult.

Health Restrictions

Pregnant guests are not permitted to swim with dolphins.

The activity is not suitable for guests with epilepsy, certain neurological or mental conditions, significant mobility difficulties or other conditions that could affect safe participation.

Personal Items & Photos

Jewellery, watches and sunscreen are not allowed during dolphin swimming.

Personal photo and video recording is prohibited during the session. Professional photographs are available for an additional fee.

Sealanya staff make the final decision on participation. The activity may be refused when safety requirements are not met. Once the program has started, withdrawal does not provide a right to a refund.
