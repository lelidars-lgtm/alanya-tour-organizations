About this experience

This full-day route combines Roman architecture, ancient coastal heritage and one of the best-known nature stops in the Manavgat region.

Aspendos is famous for its exceptionally preserved 2nd-century Roman theatre. The wider archaeological site also includes remains of the acropolis, basilica, agora, baths and ancient aqueduct system.

In Side, guests explore accessible parts of the ancient peninsula, including historic streets, the harbour area and the Temple of Apollo beside the Mediterranean. Side Theatre access depends on the current restoration status.

Manavgat Waterfall provides a relaxing nature and photo stop before the return journey.

Lunch, hotel transfer, guide service, travel insurance, the Aspendos entrance ticket and the Manavgat Waterfall entrance ticket are included.
