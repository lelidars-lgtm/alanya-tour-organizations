About This Tour

Full Day • Regional Route

Discover some of the most beautiful cultural and natural attractions of the Antalya region in a single day. This tour combines the refreshing Manavgat Waterfall, a relaxing river yacht cruise, the powerful ancient atmosphere of Aspendos and the legendary coastal city of Side.

The journey begins with a comfortable morning transfer from your hotel. Your first stop is the famous Manavgat Waterfall, one of the most visited natural landmarks in the region and a perfect place for memorable photos.

You will also enjoy a scenic yacht cruise along the Manavgat River, where the river landscape meets the Mediterranean atmosphere. The program continues with Aspendos Roman Theatre and the ancient city of Side, including the Temple of Apollo, ancient streets, columns and harbour views.
