About this experience

Eftalia Island is a large seafront leisure and aquapark area in Türkler, on the Alanya coast. The official Eftalia website describes the island as an approximately 80,000 m² beach, pool and aquapark complex built on a peninsula beside the Mediterranean.

The island combines adult and children’s pools, multiple water-slide areas, beach access, sunbathing zones, animation, sports activities, children’s facilities, restaurants and bars.

This excursion is suitable for families, couples and groups who want a full day by the sea with both active water attractions and quieter relaxation areas.

Our tour package includes transfer, entrance, buffet lunch and selected soft drinks. The exact food-and-drink service, operating slides and entertainment program depend on the selected date and the facility’s current schedule.

Eftalia Island and its aquapark operate seasonally. Slides, pools, bars, animation and service times may change because of weather, maintenance, occupancy or management decisions.
