About this experience

Catamaran Tour in Alanya combines scenic coastal cruising with swimming, music and entertainment aboard a spacious modern catamaran.

The route follows Alanya’s most recognizable shoreline, passing the Red Tower, the historic shipyard and the famous sea caves before continuing toward Cleopatra Beach, open-water viewpoints and peaceful swimming bays.

Four dedicated swimming stops give you time to enjoy the Mediterranean, while the onboard team creates a lively atmosphere with music, animation and a foam party. Buffet lunch and soft drinks are included, making this an easy full-day experience for couples, families and groups of friends.
