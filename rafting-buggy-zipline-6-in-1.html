About this experience

The 6 in 1 Combo is designed for guests who want a complete adventure day rather than a single activity. The route brings together the strongest experiences of Köprülü Canyon National Park in one full-day program from Alanya.

The core route combines a cabrio-style canyon safari, the Roman Oluk Bridge, a panoramic stop at Tazi Canyon, approximately 14 km of rafting on the Köprüçay River, a zipline section and an off-road ride by buggy or quad.

Before the active sections, participants receive a safety briefing and the required protective equipment. Rafting is organized in guided group boats, normally with an instructor, and previous rafting experience is not required.

The exact operating sequence can change according to weather, river conditions and group flow. Current price: €60 per person. Pickup time and activity-specific participation rules are confirmed for the selected date before booking.
