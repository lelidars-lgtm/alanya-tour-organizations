About this experience

The Pirate Yacht Tour in Alanya is more than a boat trip — it is a full day of swimming, music, entertainment and a joyful holiday atmosphere.

The cruise starts from Alanya Harbour, with panoramic views of the Red Tower and the historic castle.

The route continues past the historic Tersane Shipyard, Pirate Cave, Lovers Cave and Phosphorus Cave, with refreshing swimming stops along the coast.

Later, enjoy views of Cleopatra Beach and the New Harbour. With a little luck, you may see dolphins in their natural habitat.
