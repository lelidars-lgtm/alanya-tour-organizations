About this experience

Helicopter Flight in Alanya is a unique opportunity to see the most famous landmarks of the Turkish Riviera from a spectacular bird’s-eye view.

During the flight, you will enjoy breathtaking panoramas of the azure Mediterranean Sea, the historic Alanya peninsula, the majestic Taurus Mountains and the coastline that creates the atmosphere of a true luxury journey.

The route is designed to give guests maximum emotion, beautiful panoramic views and unforgettable memories.

This experience is ideal for romantic moments, proposals, luxury photo shoots, video filming and travelers who want to see Alanya from a truly exclusive angle.
