About this experience

Night Jeep Safari in Alanya is a bright alternative to the daytime heat. When the sun goes down, the adventure begins in open-top Land Rover jeeps.

The program combines panoramic viewpoints, city lights, a pirate boat cruise, music, animation and evening entertainment.

Guests enjoy a festive night atmosphere with fire show, belly dance, foam party and fun moments during the excursion.

The final part of the evening takes place at Dim River, where dinner is served in a cool mountain setting above flowing water.
