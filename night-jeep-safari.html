About This Tour

Night Jeep Safari is a bright alternative to the daytime heat. When the sun goes down, the adventure begins in open Land Rover jeeps. The route combines Alanya’s best evening viewpoints, a pirate boat cruise, entertainment shows, fireworks atmosphere and a cool riverside dinner at Dimçayı.

Tour Program

Hotel or Apartment Pick-up

Guests are collected from hotel reception or apartment address by open-top Land Rover jeeps.

Panoramic Viewpoint Above Alanya

First stop near the ancient castle walls with views of the pier, pirate ships, white yachts, Red Tower and the illuminated coastline.

Pirate Boat Cruise

A 1-hour boat tour with music, animation and festive evening atmosphere. Guests who want to swim can take swimsuit and towel.

Torch Show & Night Viewpoint

The jeep convoy climbs to a spectacular hill viewpoint where Alanya shines with thousands of lights and the night program continues with fire effects and entertainment.

Belly Dance, Fire Show & Foam Party

The excursion includes lively animation, dance performances and fun entertainment during the evening program.

Dinner at Dim River

The evening finishes in the cool mountain atmosphere of Dimçayı with dinner, music and relaxing wooden gazebos above the flowing river.
