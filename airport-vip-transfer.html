About This Service

Airport VIP Transfer is a private door-to-door transfer service between Antalya Airport, Antalya Gazipaşa–Alanya Airport and confirmed addresses in the Alanya region.

The vehicle is reserved only for the booked passengers. The final vehicle, route, pickup point, luggage capacity and price are confirmed before the transfer.

For airport arrivals, the driver or meeting representative uses the agreed meeting instructions and the transfer team monitors the supplied flight number for schedule changes.

Mercedes Vito or Sprinter-class vehicles may be assigned according to passenger count, luggage and operational availability. A specific model cannot be guaranteed unless confirmed in writing.

Transfer Program

The sequence below shows the standard booking and transfer process. Exact pickup instructions vary by airport terminal, flight and destination.

Step 1

Send Transfer Details

Provide airport, flight number, date, scheduled time, destination, passenger count, luggage and child-seat request.

Step 2

Written Confirmation

Receive the vehicle class, pickup instructions, route, confirmed price and contact information.

Step 3

Flight Monitoring

The transfer team uses the supplied flight number to follow the published arrival status and adjust the meeting timing when necessary.

Step 4

Meeting at the Confirmed Point

Follow the meeting instructions supplied for the relevant terminal, hotel, apartment or private address.

Step 5

Private Direct Transfer

Travel to the confirmed destination in the assigned private vehicle. Additional stops must be agreed in advance.
