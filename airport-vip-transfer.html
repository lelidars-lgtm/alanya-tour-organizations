About this service

Airport VIP Transfer is a private premium transfer service for guests arriving to or departing from Antalya Airport, Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport, hotels, apartments, villas and private residences in Alanya.

This is not a shared shuttle. The vehicle is reserved only for your family, couple or group. No waiting for other passengers, no unnecessary stops and no last-minute price changes.

Your professional driver meets you at the arrivals area with a personalized name sign, assists with luggage and escorts you directly to your Mercedes Vito or Sprinter vehicle.

The service is designed for comfort, privacy and punctuality: air conditioning, spacious luggage capacity, complimentary bottled water, clean premium interior and door-to-door transportation.

We monitor your flight in real time. If your flight is delayed, the pickup time is adjusted accordingly.
