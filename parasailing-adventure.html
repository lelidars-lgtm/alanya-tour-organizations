About This Experience

Parasailing Adventure is one of the most beautiful aerial experiences on the Turkish Riviera. Attached to a specially designed parachute and safely connected to a powerful speedboat, you will gently rise high above the Mediterranean Sea within seconds.

Admire Alanya Castle, the Red Tower, Cleopatra Beach, pirate ships, luxury yachts and the Taurus Mountains from an unforgettable perspective.

The flight is smooth, comfortable and suitable even for first-time participants.
