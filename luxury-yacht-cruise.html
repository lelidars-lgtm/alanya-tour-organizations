About this experience

Luxury Yacht Cruise offers a completely private journey along the beautiful Alanya coastline. The yacht is reserved exclusively for your family, partner or group of friends, with no other guests on board.

During the cruise, you will sail past some of Alanya’s most impressive coastal landmarks, including Alanya Castle, the historic city walls, famous sea caves, Cleopatra Beach, the open Mediterranean and scenic bays ideal for swimming and snorkeling.

The route and atmosphere can be tailored to your preferences. Whenever sea and weather conditions allow, the captain can adjust the cruising pace, swimming stops and time spent at each location.

The yacht is equipped with a Bluetooth audio system, allowing you to play your own music and create the perfect atmosphere. Unlimited Wi-Fi is also available on board.

One of our experienced crew members prepares fresh BBQ on board during your private cruise. Freshly prepared lunch, seasonal fruits and unlimited soft drinks are included throughout the trip.

You may also bring your own food, desserts or beverages. This is your private yacht, your time and your personal Mediterranean experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book the yacht just for two people?

Yes. The yacht is completely private. The charter price is €250 for the entire yacht for up to 5 guests, even if only two people are traveling.

How many guests are included in the price?

The charter price includes up to 5 guests. Each additional guest is €50. The yacht can comfortably accommodate up to 12 guests.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes. Private hotel pickup and return from your hotel or apartment in the Alanya region are included in the price.

What food and drinks are included?

One of our experienced crew members prepares fresh BBQ on board. Freshly prepared lunch, seasonal fruits and unlimited soft drinks are included.

Can we bring our own food or drinks?

Yes. You are welcome to bring your favorite food, desserts or beverages to make your private yacht experience even more personal.

Can we customize the route or schedule?

Yes. The captain can adjust the route, swimming stops and cruising pace according to weather conditions and your preferences whenever possible.

Are children welcome on board?

Absolutely. This private yacht cruise is ideal for couples, families and groups of friends. Life jackets are provided for adults and children.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Your safety is our highest priority. If weather or sea conditions are unsuitable, the cruise can be rescheduled for another available date at no additional cost.
