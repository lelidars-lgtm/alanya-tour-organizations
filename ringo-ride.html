About This Experience

Ringo Ride is one of the most exciting water sports activities in Alanya. It is made for guests who want speed, splashes, sharp movement and real Mediterranean adrenaline.

Guests ride authentic yellow inflatable Ringos while being pulled across the Mediterranean Sea by a powerful speed boat. Expect speed, water spray, turns and unforgettable summer emotions.

No previous training or experience is required. Before the activity starts, our experienced team provides clear safety instructions and explains how to enjoy the ride comfortably.

The activity lasts approximately 10–12 minutes and is organized for private groups with a minimum of 2 guests and up to 4 guests. Advance reservation is required.
