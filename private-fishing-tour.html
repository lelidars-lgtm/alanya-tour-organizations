About this experience

Private Fishing Tour in Alanya is designed for guests who want a peaceful day at sea with complete privacy, flexible pacing and personal attention from the captain.

Your group travels aboard a private fishing boat to selected areas along the Alanya coastline. Fishing locations are chosen according to the season, weather and sea conditions, and all essential tackle is prepared for you on board.

The experience is suitable for beginners as well as guests who already enjoy fishing. The crew provides guidance throughout the tour, while non-fishing guests can relax, enjoy the views and spend time on the Mediterranean.

Lunch and unlimited soft drinks are included. When the catch and conditions allow, fresh fish may be prepared on board. As with all fishing experiences, the catch depends on nature and cannot be guaranteed.
