About this experience

Discover the Mediterranean coastline on a full-day boat tour featuring turquoise water, quiet bays, sea caves and striking natural scenery.

The experience includes scenic swimming stops around Suluada and nearby coastal bays. Depending on the operating route, weather and sea conditions, the captain may adjust the sequence of stops or replace locations with suitable alternatives.

During the cruise, guests can relax on deck, swim in clear water and enjoy lunch on board. Hotel or apartment transfer from the Alanya region is included.

This tour is ideal for couples, families and travelers looking for a long, scenic day at sea away from the city.
