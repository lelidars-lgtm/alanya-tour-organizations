About this experience

Syedra Ancient City lies approximately 20 kilometres southeast of Alanya on a hillside overlooking the Mediterranean. The settlement traces its origins to the 7th century BC and reached its most prosperous period between the 2nd and 4th centuries AD.

The Upper City stands at an elevation of around 400 metres. Visitors enter through a monumental gate and continue to the approximately 250-metre colonnaded street, once one of the city’s main social spaces.

The archaeological site preserves Roman baths with evidence of mosaic floors, a gymnasium, workshops, numerous plastered cisterns and rock-cut religious spaces. The Cistern Cave and nearby Baptism Cave are among the most distinctive surviving features.

This tour combines archaeology, mountain scenery and wide Mediterranean views. The terrain is uneven and includes slopes, stone paths and steps, so comfortable non-slip footwear is essential.
