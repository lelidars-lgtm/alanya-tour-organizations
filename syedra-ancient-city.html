About this experience

Syedra Ancient City is one of the most atmospheric historical sites on the Alanya coastline, located just 20 km east of the city among mountain slopes overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

Unlike many popular archaeological attractions, Syedra offers a peaceful and authentic experience away from large tourist crowds. The ancient city combines impressive Roman architecture, unique archaeological remains and breathtaking panoramic viewpoints.

During the tour you will walk through ancient streets lined with columns, discover ancient mosaics, explore historic water cisterns and learn about the engineering achievements of the ancient world.

One of the highlights is the panoramic observation point located approximately 400 meters above sea level, offering spectacular views of the coastline, mountains and the Mediterranean Sea.

Syedra is a strong choice for history lovers, photographers, nature enthusiasts and travelers looking for a quieter cultural experience.
