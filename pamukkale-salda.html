About this experience

Salda Lake is one of Türkiye’s most beautiful lakes, famous for its crystal-clear turquoise water and unique white hydromagnesite sand. Its colors and atmosphere have earned it the name “the Turkish Maldives.” It is an ideal place for relaxing, walking and taking striking photographs.

Pamukkale — the “Cotton Castle” — is famous for its snow-white travertines and thermal water. It is one of Türkiye’s greatest natural wonders and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The ancient city of Hierapolis brings the classical world to life through its Roman theatre, ancient streets, columns, historic ruins and panoramic views.

Guests may also visit the famous Cleopatra Pool, filled with warm mineral water and ancient submerged columns.

This journey combines natural wonders, ancient history, relaxation, beautiful photo locations and the atmosphere of a premium trip through Türkiye.

The tour is ideal for families, couples, nature and history lovers, and travelers who value beautiful places and a comfortable itinerary.
