About This Experience

Professional Wakeboarding is a powerful and exciting water sport for guests who want action, speed and a real challenge on the Mediterranean Sea. You ride on a wakeboard while being pulled by a speedboat, learning balance, control and smooth movement over the water.

Before the activity begins, experienced guides explain safety rules, body position, handle control and how to enjoy the ride with confidence. The session lasts approximately 20 minutes.

This is an individual water sport. Private lessons with professional instructors are available for guests who want deeper training and faster progress.
