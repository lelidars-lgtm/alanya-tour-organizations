About this experience

Gazipasa Bays is an exclusive journey through the hidden paradises of Gazipasa, combining ancient history, natural wonders and time to relax in exceptionally beautiful bays far from mass tourism.

This tour is designed for travelers who appreciate atmospheric locations, turquoise water, ancient ruins and the authentic natural beauty of Turkey.

Delik Deniz — King’s Bay is one of the most beautiful and unusual places on the coast, with crystal-clear turquoise water and a giant natural rock arch opening directly toward the sea.

Antiochia ad Cragum is an ancient city located high above the sea amid dramatic landscapes. Its ruins, columns and remains of old streets create the feeling of traveling back in time.

Aytap / Iotape is a unique coastal archaeological site where the history of the ancient world meets the blue Mediterranean Sea.

Yalan Dünya Cave is an impressive natural cave with stalactites and stalagmites, hidden among the rocks and landscapes of the region.

This journey is a wonderful alternative to classic sightseeing tours and reveals a completely different side of the Alanya coastline.
