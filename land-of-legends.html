About The Land of Legends

The Land of Legends in Belek is much more than a traditional amusement park. It brings together themed entertainment lands, water attractions, family zones, roller coasters, character experiences, live shows, restaurants and Shopping Avenue in one large resort-style destination.

Families with children can explore colourful areas inspired by Nickelodeon characters, PAW Patrol, SpongeBob, Masha and the Bear and other themed worlds. At the same time, teenagers and adults can choose faster rides, major roller coasters, high-energy slides and adrenaline attractions.

Water entertainment is spread across areas such as Aqua Land, Tropic Lagoon and other seasonal water zones, with pools, slides, family experiences and more intense attractions. Not every attraction is suitable for every guest: height, age, weight and safety restrictions apply individually.

In the evening, Shopping Avenue becomes a separate entertainment setting with illuminated canals, restaurants, shops, fountain effects and seasonal live events. The current program may include the Musical Boat Parade, character appearances, music, light effects and stage performances.

The exact list of operating lands, attractions, water areas and evening events depends on the selected date, weather, maintenance and the official daily program of The Land of Legends.

Important Park Rules

Food & Drinks

Outside food and alcoholic or non-alcoholic drinks, including water, are not permitted inside the Theme Park. Bags may be checked at the entrance.

Swim Diapers

Babies and children under 3 must wear waterproof swim diapers in pool areas. Standard diapers are not suitable.

Children Under 12

Guests under 12 may enter and use the park only when accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over.

Ride Restrictions

Each ride and slide has its own health, height, weight, age and accompaniment requirements. Entry is allowed only when all posted safety conditions are met.

Guests must follow the information signs and staff instructions at every attraction. Rules and operating conditions may be changed by The Land of Legends.
