About this experience

The Land of Legends in Belek brings together a theme park, water attractions, family zones, roller coasters and seasonal live entertainment.

The Day Program is designed for guests who want access to the operating Theme Park lands and attractions. The Night Show option is a separate evening trip to Shopping Avenue, where the current entertainment program may include a musical boat parade, light effects and live performances.

Operating attractions, water areas and event times depend on the selected date, weather, maintenance and the park’s official program.
