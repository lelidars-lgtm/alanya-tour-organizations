About this experience

The Land of Legends is the largest theme park in Türkiye, combining an amusement park, waterpark and spectacular evening entertainment.

It is ideal for families with children and adults who want an active, colorful and memorable day in one of Antalya’s most famous entertainment destinations.

You can choose a day program, a night show, or a full day with evening show.

Highlights

World-class roller coasters and family rides
Water slides, pools and lazy river
Evening parade with performers and characters
Water show with boats and light installations
Music, lights, dance performances and festive atmosphere
Transfer, entrance and insurance included
