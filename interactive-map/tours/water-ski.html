About This Experience

Water Ski Experience is a classic Mediterranean challenge built around speed, balance and technique.

After a clear safety briefing, you enter the water with professional equipment and are pulled behind the boat. Beginners receive practical guidance on body position, safe falling and the correct water start, while more confident riders can focus on control and smooth turns.

One booked session lasts approximately 15 minutes and includes your attempts during the allocated riding period. The activity is individual; groups reserve consecutive sessions.

Extended private instruction may be available at an additional cost and must be confirmed in advance.
