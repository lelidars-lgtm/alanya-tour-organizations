About this experience

Horse Riding / Horse Farm is a calm nature experience in Alanya for guests who want to discover a different side of the region: mountain trails, fresh air, countryside landscapes and panoramic views.

The experience begins with transfer from your hotel or apartment to the horse farm. On arrival, the instructor introduces the route and safety rules, helps with equipment and prepares guests for a short trial ride.

The main route follows scenic mountain roads and natural trails. Depending on operational conditions, the program may include countryside viewpoints, peaceful village surroundings and stops for rest and photographs.

No previous riding experience is required. Participation and horse selection are confirmed on site according to the guest’s age, height, confidence and the instructor’s safety assessment.

Morning departures are usually the most comfortable during the warmer months. Exact pickup and return times may vary depending on hotel location, traffic and route conditions.

Important Horse Riding Safety Rules

Participation is subject to the horse club’s final safety assessment. Horse selection, route access, riding format and permission to continue are determined according to the rider’s age, height, weight, balance and experience, as well as horse welfare, equipment fit and current conditions.

Instructor Instructions

Guests must attend the full briefing and follow the instructor during mounting, the trial ride, the route, stops and dismounting. Riding away from the group or ignoring commands is not permitted.

Helmet, Clothing & Footwear

A correctly fitted riding helmet must remain fastened while mounted. Long trousers and closed secure footwear are required; riding or ankle boots with a small heel are preferred. Loose footwear and unsecured items are prohibited.

Behaviour Around Horses

Approach horses calmly from a visible side, keep a safe distance from other horses and never stand directly behind one. Running, shouting, sudden movements, feeding or touching horses without permission are not allowed.

Health, Route & Conditions

Pregnant guests cannot participate. Riders must disclose relevant health or mobility concerns. The instructor may shorten, change or cancel the route because of weather, terrain, horse condition or rider safety.

The horse club’s briefing, helmet rules, rider assessment, horse allocation and instructor decisions always take priority over the advertised route, timing and general participation information.
