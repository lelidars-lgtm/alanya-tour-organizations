About this experience

Family Buggy Safari in Alanya is an exciting off-road adventure for travelers who enjoy active experiences, speed, and unforgettable emotions.

The tour combines thrilling experiences, beautiful natural scenery, and the atmosphere of a real adventure among mountains, forest roads, and picturesque routes along the Mediterranean coast.

The Family Buggy Safari follows forest trails, mountain roads, dusty and muddy tracks, and natural routes far from crowded tourist areas.

After the transfer to the safari base, you will receive a detailed safety briefing, an introduction to the buggy controls, protective equipment, and a test drive of approximately 15 minutes.

No previous buggy-driving experience is required. The instructors will help you become comfortable and confident before the main route begins.

During the route, there is a stop by a scenic mountain river where you can relax, cool down, and take memorable photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a driving licence?
A:

A B1 driving licence is required to independently drive a qualifying buggy on public roads in Turkey. Requirements for this guided off-road tour may differ depending on the vehicle classification and operator rules, so please confirm them when booking.

Q: Can beginners drive a buggy?
A:

Yes. No previous buggy-driving experience is required. A safety briefing and approximately 15-minute test drive are provided before the main route.

Q: Can children join the tour?
A:

Yes. Children aged 5–11 can ride as the second passenger in a shared buggy. Guests aged 12 and older are charged at the adult rate.

Q: Is the tour suitable during pregnancy?
A:

No. Buggy riding during pregnancy is strongly discouraged because the route may include vibration, sudden movement, uneven ground, mud, and off-road impacts.

Q: Will our clothes get dusty or wet?
A:

Yes. The route may include dusty, muddy, and wet sections. Wear comfortable clothes that can get dirty and bring a bandana, sunglasses, and a change of clothes if needed.

Q: What happens in bad weather?
A:

The route may be adjusted, delayed, or cancelled if weather conditions make the tour unsafe. Final information is provided before departure.

Q: Is hotel transfer included?
A:

Yes. Pickup and return transfer from your hotel or apartment are included. The exact pickup time is confirmed after booking.

Important Family Buggy Safari Safety Rules

Although this is presented as a family experience, the route includes vibration, uneven terrain, mud, dust and sudden movement. Participation, driver approval, passenger seating and route access remain subject to the operator’s final safety assessment.

Briefing & Instructor Rules

Every driver must attend the briefing and complete the test drive. Guests must follow the instructor, keep their place in the group and stop immediately when directed.

Children & Passenger Seating

Children may ride only in the passenger position approved by the operator. They must remain seated, use the fitted restraint and stay under continuous adult supervision throughout the route and stops.

Protective Equipment & Clothing

Use all protective equipment supplied by the operator. Wear closed secure footwear and practical clothing. Loose scarves, jewellery, phones and unsecured belongings must not interfere with driving or moving parts.

River Stop, Weather & Route

At the river stop, children must remain with an adult and avoid slippery rocks or moving water. The operator may change, shorten, delay or cancel the route because of weather, terrain, vehicle condition or participant safety.

The operator’s briefing, vehicle-specific rules, child-passenger approval and instructor decisions always take priority over the advertised route, timing, river stop and general participation information.
