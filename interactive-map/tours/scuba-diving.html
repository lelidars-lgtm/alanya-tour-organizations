About This Experience

Scuba Diving in Alanya is a guided yacht-based diving experience with separate arrangements for beginners and certified divers.

First-time participants receive a briefing, equipment introduction and shallow-water skills practice before any open-water dive. They remain under direct professional supervision.

Certified divers may follow a different plan based on certification level, recent dive history, equipment, sea conditions and the dive professional’s approval.

The standard program is planned as three progressive dives at approximately 3–6 m, 7–9 m and 10–12 m. The actual number of dives, depth and underwater route depend on certification, health screening, participant readiness, sea conditions and the instructor’s final decision.
