About this experience

Family Jeep Safari is a private jeep journey and an opportunity to discover the authentic side of Alanya beyond standard tourist routes.

You will drive through mountain roads and authentic villages of Antalya Province, stop at picturesque waterfalls, and enjoy panoramic views of Green Canyon and five surrounding mountain ranges.

Optionally, you can explore an ancient cave with winding passages and a unique historical atmosphere.

This tour is perfect for families, friends, and travelers who want to combine nature, light adventure, spectacular scenery, and comfortable travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

The route is organized as a private jeep experience. Final vehicle allocation and route details are confirmed during booking according to the group size.

How is the price calculated?

The adult price depends on the total number of guests: €35 per person for 5–9 guests, €30 for 10–14 guests, and €25 for groups of 15 or more.

How much do children pay?

Children aged 5–11 pay 50% of the applicable group price. Guests aged 12 and over pay the full applicable group price.

Are lunch and transfer included?

Yes. Hotel or apartment transfer, a professional driver, insurance and lunch are included. Drinks, personal expenses and optional cave admission are not included.

What time does the tour start?

This is a private-format route. The exact pickup and departure time is confirmed after booking.

Is the cave visit mandatory?

No. The ancient cave visit is optional and its entrance fee is paid separately.

Important Family Jeep Safari Safety Rules

This is a family-oriented private jeep route, but it still includes mountain roads, uneven surfaces, outdoor stops and changing weather conditions. The driver’s instructions and the safety needs of children always take priority over the advertised route and schedule.

Safe Behaviour in the Jeep

Guests must remain seated while the vehicle is moving, keep arms and personal items inside, avoid standing or changing seats, and follow the driver during all sections of the mountain route.

Children & Supervision

Children must stay under continuous adult supervision. Seating arrangements and any required child restraint must be confirmed before departure according to the child’s age and the assigned vehicle.

Waterfalls, Viewpoints & Stops

At waterfalls, viewpoints and roadside stops, stay within the area indicated by the driver. Children must not approach steep edges, slippery rocks, moving water or road traffic without an adult.

Weather, Roads & Route Changes

The driver may change, shorten, delay or cancel parts of the route because of rain, heat, road conditions, traffic, vehicle safety, local restrictions or the needs of children in the group.

The driver’s instructions, vehicle-specific seating rules and decisions made for child and passenger safety always take priority over the advertised stops, timings and optional activities.
