About this experience

Sapadere Canyon is a refreshing journey into the Taurus Mountains, approximately 40–45 kilometres from central Alanya. The route combines mountain landscapes, waterfalls, clear river water and the peaceful atmosphere of a natural canyon.

The main experience is a walk along the wooden pathway built through the canyon. The route passes between dramatic rock walls, green vegetation, small waterfalls and clear mountain pools, creating excellent opportunities for photographs and a relaxing nature break.

The tour also introduces guests to the traditional character of the Sapadere region through a village stop and local cultural points along the route. Depending on the day’s program, these may include a silk workshop, peanut plantation or historic village mosque.

An optional visit to Dwarf Cave, also known as Cüceler Cave, is available for guests who would like to see natural stalactite formations and atmospheric rock chambers. The cave entrance costs €3 per person and is paid separately.

After exploring the canyon, guests enjoy lunch in a natural setting. Free time is provided for relaxing, taking photographs and swimming where permitted and conditions allow.

This tour is suitable for families, couples and travellers who want to discover a quieter, greener side of the Alanya region away from the busy coastline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sapadere Canyon suitable for children?

Yes. The tour is suitable for families, but children should be supervised closely on the wooden pathway and near the water.

Is the Dwarf Cave entrance included?

No. Entrance to Dwarf Cave / Cüceler Cave costs €3 per person and is paid separately.

Is swimming guaranteed?

No. Swimming is possible only where permitted and when weather, water and safety conditions allow.

What shoes should I wear?

Comfortable non-slip walking shoes are strongly recommended because some surfaces may be wet or uneven.

Can the cultural stops change?

Yes. Depending on the day’s route, the program may include a silk workshop, peanut plantation or village mosque. Their order and availability may vary.

Are pickup and return times exact?

No. Times are approximate and may vary depending on your hotel location, traffic conditions and the day’s route. The exact pickup time is confirmed after booking.
