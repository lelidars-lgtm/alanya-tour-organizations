About this experience

Green Canyon is a peaceful and scenic journey to one of the most beautiful natural locations in the Mediterranean region. The majestic Taurus Mountains, emerald water and a deeply relaxing atmosphere create the perfect day away from the city.

The experience takes place as a comfortable sightseeing cruise across one of Türkiye’s most beautiful reservoirs. The route is surrounded by dense forests, rocky shores and panoramic natural landscapes.

After pickup from your hotel, you travel toward Green Canyon. The journey takes approximately two hours each way. A professional tour guide accompanies the group and shares information about the history, nature and character of the region.

On arrival, you board a comfortable sightseeing boat and begin a cruise across the turquoise reservoir among mountain landscapes. The program includes swimming stops, time to relax outdoors, fishing from the boat and panoramic island views.

Lunch is served at a restaurant by the canyon with a beautiful panoramic view. Main-course choices include chicken, fish or meatballs, accompanied by a side dish and unlimited soft drinks according to the program.

This tour is ideal for families with children, couples and guests who enjoy nature, peaceful surroundings, beautiful photography and a relaxed day outdoors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Green Canyon suitable for children?

Yes. The cruise is calm and family-friendly. Parents should supervise children closely during swimming stops and while moving around the boat.

Is swimming compulsory?

No. Swimming is optional. Guests may remain on the boat and enjoy the scenery during the stops.

Are fishing rods included?

Fishing equipment is provided on board according to availability and the tour program.

What food and drinks are included?

Lunch includes a choice of chicken, fish or meatballs with a side dish. Soft drinks are included according to the program.

How long is the transfer?

The drive takes approximately two hours each way, depending on your pickup area and traffic conditions.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing and a phone for contact.

Is the local zoo entrance included?

No. Zoo entrance costs €5 per person. Photos with selected animals are available for an additional fee and depend on availability and zoo rules.
