About this experience

Discover two of Türkiye’s most extraordinary landscapes in one remarkable journey from Alanya.

The first major stop is Salda Lake, a protected high-alkaline lake surrounded by pine-covered landscapes and famous for its exceptionally clear turquoise water and bright white mineral shoreline. Often called the “Turkish Maldives,” Salda is far more than a beautiful photo location: its magnesium-rich geological formations have attracted scientific interest because of their similarities to mineral structures studied in Mars’s Jezero Crater.

The lake lies within a Special Environmental Protection Area created to preserve its rare geological formations, water quality and biodiversity. During the stop, guests can enjoy the scenery, walk in permitted areas and take photographs. Access to the shoreline and swimming may vary depending on current environmental protection rules and the section visited.

The journey continues to Pamukkale, where calcite-rich thermal waters have created dazzling white terraces, petrified cascades and natural basins above the surrounding plain.

Above the travertines stands Hierapolis, an ancient Greco-Roman thermal city founded by the kings of Pergamon in the late second century BC. The site includes a monumental Roman theatre, ancient baths, temples, streets, a vast necropolis and important early Christian monuments.

Pamukkale and Hierapolis together form a UNESCO World Heritage Site, combining an exceptional natural landscape with one of Anatolia’s most important ancient thermal settlements.

When booked with our agency, entrance tickets to Pamukkale, Hierapolis and the Archaeology Museum are already included in the stated tour price.

The journey also includes hotel transfer, guide service, insurance and meals according to the confirmed program. Because this is a long-distance full-day tour with a very early departure, guests should be prepared for extended travel time.
