About this experience

Luxury Yacht Cruise offers a completely private journey along the Alanya coastline. The yacht is reserved exclusively for your family, partner or group of friends, with no other guests on board.

During the cruise, you will sail past Alanya Castle, the historic city walls, famous sea caves, Cleopatra Beach and scenic Mediterranean bays.

The experience combines coastal sightseeing with time to relax on the sun terrace or in the shaded lounge. Swimming, snorkeling and use of the SUP board are available when sea conditions and the captain permit.

The yacht has a Bluetooth audio system and Wi-Fi, allowing your group to create its own private atmosphere on board.

Fresh BBQ, prepared lunch, seasonal fruits and unlimited soft drinks are included. The private format is suitable for romantic cruises, birthdays, family celebrations and sunset gatherings.

Important Luxury Yacht Safety Rules

All guests must follow the captain and crew. These rules apply during boarding, movement on deck, water activities and changing sea or weather conditions.

Captain & Crew Instructions

Guests must follow all instructions during boarding, movement on deck, swimming stops, use of equipment, meal service and return to the harbour.

Lifejackets, Capacity & Children

The permitted guest capacity must not be exceeded. Suitable life-saving equipment must remain available, and children require continuous adult supervision and appropriate lifejackets.

Swimming, SUP & Deck Safety

Swimming and SUP use are allowed only with crew permission. Running, unsafe climbing, diving from unapproved areas and entering the water while the yacht is moving are prohibited.

Weather, Sea & Route Changes

The captain may delay, shorten, reroute, reschedule or cancel the cruise when wind, waves, visibility, technical issues or port restrictions affect safe operation.

The captain’s safety decision, vessel-specific briefing and onboard rules always take priority over the advertised route, swimming stops, timetable and entertainment plan.
