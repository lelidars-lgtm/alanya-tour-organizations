About this experience

Demre – Myra – Kekova combines the ancient heritage of Lycia, the story of Saint Nicholas and the Mediterranean scenery of Kekova in one full-day journey from Alanya.

At Myra Ancient City, you will see the famous Lycian rock-cut tombs and the remains of the Roman theatre. The route also includes the St. Nicholas Museum, traditionally associated with Saint Nicholas, Bishop of Myra.

The second part of the excursion includes a boat trip along Kekova. The boat passes beside partially submerged ancient remains and ruins preserved among the coastal rocks. Visibility depends on sea and weather conditions.

A swimming stop is included according to the boat route and current sea conditions. The excursion is suitable for families, including guests travelling with young children.
