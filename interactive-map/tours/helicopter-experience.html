Important Helicopter Experience Safety Rules

Crew Instructions

Passengers must attend the safety briefing and follow all pilot and ground-crew instructions during approach, boarding, flight, landing and disembarkation.

Approaching the Helicopter

Approach only when instructed, use the route shown by the crew and never walk behind the helicopter or near the tail rotor.

Loose Items & Equipment

Secure hats, scarves, bags, jewellery, phones, selfie sticks and camera straps. Carry personal items low and never raise objects above your head near the aircraft.

Weather & Operational Conditions

The pilot may delay, reroute, reschedule or cancel the flight when weather, technical or operational conditions do not allow safe operation.

Safety has priority over the advertised schedule and route. The operator’s aircraft-specific briefing, safety card and crew instructions always take priority.
