About this experience

This full-day excursion combines archaeology, coastal heritage and nature in one balanced route through the Antalya region.

Aspendos is best known for its exceptionally preserved 2nd-century Roman theatre, associated through its inscriptions with the architect Zenon, son of Theodorus. The wider archaeological site also preserves remains of the agora, basilica, baths, acropolis and an important ancient aqueduct system.

In Side, guests explore the atmosphere of the ancient peninsula, its monumental streets and the Temple of Apollo beside the Mediterranean. Side Theatre is currently closed to visitors because of restoration, so it is presented from accessible exterior areas unless official access conditions change.

Kurşunlu Waterfall offers a contrasting nature stop among pine forest, shaded paths and freshwater scenery.

The order and duration of visits may change according to traffic, weather, site access, restoration work, special events and the confirmed daily program.
