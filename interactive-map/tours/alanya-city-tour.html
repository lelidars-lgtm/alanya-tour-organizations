About this experience

Alanya Sunset City Tour is an afternoon and evening route combining the city’s panoramic terrace, castle district, cable car and famous western coastline.

The bus makes confirmed stops at the Alanya panoramic terrace and in the castle area before continuing to Cleopatra Beach and the Damlataş district.

A round-trip cable-car ticket is included. The cable car connects the Damlataş area with the upper castle district and offers elevated views of Cleopatra Beach, the city and the Mediterranean.

At Cleopatra Beach, guests receive free time for relaxing and seasonal swimming, subject to weather and sea conditions. Damlataş Cave can be visited during the stop; its entrance fee is paid separately unless otherwise confirmed.

The exact route order and timing may change according to season, sunset time, weather, traffic, cable-car operation and local access conditions.
