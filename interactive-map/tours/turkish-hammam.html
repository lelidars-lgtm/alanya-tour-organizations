About This Experience

Turkish Hammam is a classic 90-minute wellness program built around traditional heat, cleansing and massage stages.

The standard package combines sauna or warm-room preparation, steam, traditional kese exfoliation, foam massage and an approximately 20-minute classic massage.

The exact order and duration of individual stages may vary according to the hammam schedule, guest condition and selected package, while the total program remains approximately 90 minutes.

Guests should inform the hammam in advance about pregnancy, recent surgery, skin irritation, allergies, cardiovascular conditions, high blood pressure, injuries or other health concerns that may affect heat exposure, peeling or massage.

Frequently Asked Questions

The standard hammam package lasts approximately 90 minutes. Transfer and waiting time may increase the total time away from the hotel.

The standard package includes the hammam entrance, heat and steam preparation, kese peeling, foam massage and an approximately 20-minute classic massage. Transfer zone and insurance must be confirmed before booking.

Pregnancy must be disclosed before booking. Sauna, steam, peeling and massage may be restricted or excluded, and the hammam may request medical approval.

Children’s admission, permitted stages and price depend on age and hammam policy. These details must be confirmed before booking.

Kese peeling removes surface dead skin, but freshly treated skin may be more sensitive. Avoid immediate strong sun exposure and follow the hammam’s skincare guidance.

Transfer is included only for the confirmed service zone and booking option. Other locations may require an additional charge or direct arrival.
