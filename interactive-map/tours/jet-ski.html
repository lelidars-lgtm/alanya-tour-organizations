About This Experience

Jet Ski Safari is one of the fastest ways to experience the Mediterranean coast of Alanya.

After a short safety briefing, you take control of the jet ski and ride within the designated activity area under the supervision of the water-sports team.

No previous jet ski experience is normally required. The team explains acceleration, steering, stopping distance, safe passenger position and the signals used on the water.

Choose the available 130 HP or 200 HP option and a 10- or 15-minute session. Final machine allocation, passenger configuration and route depend on operator approval, weather and sea conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

No previous experience is normally required. You must attend the briefing, demonstrate that you understand the controls and follow the staff instructions throughout the ride.

One passenger may be allowed depending on the selected machine, combined weight, sea conditions and the operator’s final approval.

The booked riding period is 10 or 15 minutes according to the selected tariff. Briefing, equipment preparation and transfer time are separate.

The activity may be delayed, shortened, rescheduled or cancelled when wind, waves or visibility do not allow safe operation.

The operator confirms the minimum driver age, passenger age, identification requirements and any local restrictions before booking. Minors may require a parent or legal guardian.

Transfer is included only for the confirmed service zone and booking option. Availability and any extra charge for other areas are confirmed before payment.
