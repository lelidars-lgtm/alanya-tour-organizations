About This Experience

VIP Speed Boat Tour is a private 90-minute sea experience for groups of up to eight guests.

The boat is reserved for your group, creating a more personal atmosphere than a shared excursion. The route and speed are adjusted by the captain according to sea conditions, local restrictions and the selected time slot.

A swimming stop may be included when conditions and timing allow. It is not guaranteed at a specific bay, and the captain makes the final safety decision.

The listed price is for the private boat session, not per person. Advance reservation is recommended, especially for sunset and summer time slots.
