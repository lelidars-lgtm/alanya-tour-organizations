About this experience

Paragliding in Alanya is one of the most exciting ways to see the Turkish Riviera from above and feel the true freedom of flight.

During the flight, you will see the famous Cleopatra Beach, Alanya Castle and the historic peninsula, the azure Mediterranean Sea, the majestic Taurus Mountains and the panoramic coastline from up to 800 meters.

The flight is performed in tandem with a professional certified pilot, so the experience is suitable even for guests who have never tried paragliding before.

Before take-off, you receive a detailed safety briefing. Then you take off smoothly from the mountain launch area and enjoy breathtaking views over Alanya.

Important Paragliding Safety Rules

Pilot Instructions

Guests must follow the pilot’s instructions during preparation, take-off, flight and landing. Final participation approval belongs to the pilot.

Clothing & Equipment

Wear comfortable sports clothing and secure closed shoes. Loose items, jewellery and unsecured phones must be removed or safely stored.

Health Restrictions

The activity may not be suitable during pregnancy or for guests with serious heart, back, neck, neurological or mobility conditions, recent surgery or severe fear of heights.

Weather & Flight Conditions

Flight time, launch point, route and landing location may change. Unsafe wind or weather conditions may cause delay, rescheduling or cancellation.

Safety always has priority over the advertised schedule. The pilot and operator make the final decision regarding participation, launch and landing conditions.
