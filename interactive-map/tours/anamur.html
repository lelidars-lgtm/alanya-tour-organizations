About this experience

Mamure Castle and Anemurium create one of the most distinctive historical routes on Türkiye’s eastern Mediterranean coast. The excursion combines medieval fortifications, a major ancient port settlement, coastal scenery and the agricultural landscape of Anamur.

Mamure Castle stands approximately six kilometres east of Anamur. It was built over earlier Roman foundations and developed through Byzantine, Seljuk, Karamanid and Ottoman periods. The fortress includes defensive walls, towers, courtyards, cisterns, a historic mosque and a surrounding moat.

Anemurium was an important coastal settlement known from at least the 4th century BC. Its surviving remains include city walls, Roman baths, a theatre, an odeon, a palestra, churches and an extensive necropolis.

The program also includes a stop in Anamur’s banana-growing landscape and seasonal swimming or seaside free time, depending on weather, sea conditions and the confirmed daily route.

The order and duration of visits may change according to seasonal opening hours, site access, traffic, weather and the local operating program.
