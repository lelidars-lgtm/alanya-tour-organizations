About This Experience

Parasailing Adventure is one of the most spectacular ways to see Alanya from above.

You take off smoothly from the rear platform of a specially equipped speedboat and rise above the turquoise Mediterranean. As the coastline opens beneath you, the noise fades and the experience becomes calm, weightless and panoramic.

From the air you may see Cleopatra Beach, Alanya Castle, the harbour, passing boats and the Taurus Mountains. No previous experience is required: the crew prepares the equipment, explains the position for takeoff and landing, and controls the flight from the boat.

Solo and tandem flights are available. A triple flight may be possible only after the captain confirms the combined weight, wind and sea conditions.

Tour Program

The sequence remains the same, while exact pickup and flight times may change because of wind, sea conditions, boat traffic and the operator’s schedule.

Pickup

Hotel or Apartment Transfer

Pickup from the confirmed service area and transfer to the water-sports departure point.

Arrival

Boat Check-in

Meet the crew, confirm the selected flight option and prepare for boarding.

Briefing

Safety Instructions and Equipment

The crew fits the harness and life jacket and explains takeoff, flight position and landing.

Departure

Boat Ride and Preparation

The boat moves into the operating area while the crew prepares the parasail and flight line.

Flight

Parasailing Above Alanya

Rise smoothly above the sea and enjoy panoramic views for approximately 10–15 minutes on the standard flight.

Landing

Controlled Boat-Deck Landing

The crew reels the line in gradually and guides you back onto the boat platform.

Return

Return Transfer

After the boat returns, transfer is provided back to your hotel or apartment in the included zone.

Safety has priority over the advertised schedule. The captain may change the flight configuration, delay the activity or stop operations because of wind, sea or equipment conditions.
