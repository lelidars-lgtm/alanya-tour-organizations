About this experience

Istanbul by Plane is a full-day cultural journey from Alanya to Türkiye’s largest city and former imperial capital. The tour is designed for guests who want to experience the Historic Peninsula, major monuments and the Bosphorus without a long road journey.

The program combines round-trip domestic flights, airport and hotel transfers, guide service, meals, entrance tickets and a shared Bosphorus sightseeing cruise. Istanbul’s historic areas preserve landmarks from the Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman periods, creating one of the world’s most distinctive cityscapes.

Visits may include Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace and other confirmed attractions in the Historic Peninsula. Access and the order of visits may change because of flight schedules, traffic, prayer times, attraction opening conditions and the local partner’s daily program.

The Bosphorus cruise offers views of waterfront palaces, historic mansions, fortresses, bridges and the meeting point of Europe and Asia.
