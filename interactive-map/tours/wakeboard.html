About This Experience

Wakeboarding combines speed, balance and technique in one active Mediterranean session.

You ride on a wakeboard while being pulled by a speedboat. Before entering the water, the team explains the start position, handle control, body alignment, safe falling and the signals used by the boat crew.

The booked session lasts approximately 20 minutes. For beginners, this period may include several start attempts and short riding intervals rather than 20 minutes of uninterrupted standing on the board.

Private instruction may be available on request, but its duration, price and instructor availability must be confirmed before booking.
