About This Experience

E-Foil Experience & Training combines modern technology, balance and the feeling of flying just above the Mediterranean Sea.

The electric motor drives a hydrofoil beneath the board. As speed increases, the board rises above the surface, creating a quiet and smooth gliding sensation that is completely different from a conventional board sport.

Beginners receive step-by-step guidance before entering the water. The 25-minute option is suitable for guests with previous board-sport experience or those choosing a short introduction. The 40-minute course provides more instructor support and practice time.
