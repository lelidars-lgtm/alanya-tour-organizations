About this experience

Night Jeep Safari is an evening combination tour that brings together an open-top jeep route, panoramic stops above Alanya, a sunset or night boat cruise and dinner by Dim River.

The boat program normally includes music, animation and a foam party. A swimming break may be offered when sea, weather and operational conditions are suitable.

After the coastal section, the jeeps continue through the city and mountain roads toward Dim River, where dinner and the evening entertainment program take place.

This is an active group excursion rather than a quiet sightseeing tour. Music, water, foam and lively entertainment are part of the experience.

The exact pickup time, boat duration, swimming stop, shows and return time may change according to hotel location, traffic, weather, sea conditions and the operator’s program for the selected date.

Important Safari Safety Rules

Children & Supervision

Children must remain beside a parent or responsible adult. Standing, changing seats or leaning outside the vehicle while it is moving is not permitted.

Jeep Safety

All passengers must follow the driver’s instructions. Keep hands, feet, phones and personal belongings inside the vehicle. Enter or leave the jeep only after it has stopped completely.

Health Restrictions

The route includes uneven mountain roads. It may not be suitable during pregnancy or for guests with serious back, neck or heart conditions, recent surgery or severe motion sickness.

Route & Weather Conditions

The route, stops and duration may change because of weather, road conditions, traffic or the driver’s safety decision. Difficult sections may be adjusted or omitted.

Guests must follow the driver’s and guide’s instructions throughout the tour. The final decision regarding route safety and participation belongs to the tour operator and driver.
