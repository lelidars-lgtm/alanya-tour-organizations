About This Experience

Ringo Ride is a private towable-water activity for groups of two to four participants.

Guests ride on inflatable ringos pulled by a motorboat, holding the safety handles while the crew controls the route, speed and turns.

No previous experience is normally required. Before departure, the team explains the correct riding position, grip, safe behavior during turns and what to do if someone falls into the water.

The booked ride lasts approximately 10–12 minutes. Intensity is selected by the boat crew according to the group, sea conditions and safety requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

The page presents €140 as the private Ringo Ride price for a group of two to four participants.

The stated minimum is two participants. Availability for one person must be confirmed separately before booking.

No previous experience is normally required. Everyone must attend the briefing, wear a life jacket and follow the crew’s instructions.

The crew stops or adjusts the activity and follows its recovery procedure. Participants must remain calm and follow the signals explained during the briefing.

The activity may be delayed, shortened, rescheduled or cancelled when wind, waves or visibility do not allow safe operation.

Transfer is included only for the confirmed service zone and booking option. Other areas may require an extra charge or direct arrival.
