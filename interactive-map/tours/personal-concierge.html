About This Service

Personal Concierge is a private chauffeur and journey-coordination service arranged around an individual itinerary.

The service can be requested for business appointments, shopping, restaurant evenings, family travel, airport connections, intercity journeys and transportation to a clinic or medical appointment.

It provides transportation and route coordination only. It does not include medical care, interpreting, legal representation, security services or unrestricted personal assistance unless a separate service is specifically confirmed.

The route, service hours, waiting time, vehicle class, passenger capacity, luggage allowance and final price are confirmed before booking. Long-distance and intercity requests remain subject to vehicle, driver and route availability.
