About This Experience

Stand Up Paddleboarding is a calm guided sea activity designed for both first-time and experienced paddlers.

The session begins with instruction on board position, balance, paddle grip, turning, stopping and safe entry into the water.

The experience lasts approximately one hour. The exact route and whether Red Tower or castle views are reached depend on the departure point, wind, currents, visibility and the instructor’s safety decision.

Sunrise or early-morning sessions may be available, but the exact time must be confirmed before booking and cannot be guaranteed every day.
