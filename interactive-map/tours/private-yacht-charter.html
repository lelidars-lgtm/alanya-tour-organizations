About This Experience

Private Yacht Charter in Alanya is an individually planned sea experience for couples, families and private groups.

It can be arranged for marriage proposals, birthdays, anniversaries, sunset experiences, family celebrations, photo sessions and selected coastal routes.

The yacht, route, duration, departure point, guest capacity, catering, decorations, music and transfer are confirmed before booking. There is no guaranteed universal route or unlimited destination choice.

Swimming, anchoring and route changes depend on sea conditions, local restrictions and the captain’s decision. Alcoholic beverages or celebratory drinks are subject to yacht policy, legal requirements and written confirmation.

Important Private Yacht Safety Rules

Captain Instructions

All guests must follow the captain and crew during boarding, movement on deck, swimming, anchoring and emergency procedures.

Lifejackets & Capacity

The yacht must carry suitable life-saving equipment for the permitted number of people. Child-size lifejackets must be confirmed when children join.

Swimming & Deck Safety

Swimming is allowed only with crew permission. Running, unsafe climbing, diving from unapproved areas and entering the water while the vessel is moving are prohibited.

Weather & Route Changes

The captain may change or cancel the route, swimming stop, timing or entire charter when sea, weather, port or technical conditions require it.

The captain’s safety decision, vessel-specific briefing and onboard instructions always take priority over the advertised route, timing and event plan.
