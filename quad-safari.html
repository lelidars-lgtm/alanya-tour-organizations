About this experience

Quad Safari in Alanya is an exciting off-road adventure for guests who enjoy speed, nature and active experiences.

Your trip begins with a comfortable transfer to the quad safari base. Professional instructors explain the safety rules, provide protective helmets and help every guest become familiar with the controls.

A short test drive is included before the main route, so the experience is suitable even for first-time riders.

The route follows forest tracks, mountain roads, dusty and muddy sections and scenic areas near the river, with stops for rest, photographs and swimming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hotel transfer included?

Yes. Pickup from your hotel or apartment and return transfer are included.

Do I need previous quad experience?

No. A safety briefing and test drive are included before the main route.

Who may drive the quad?

The driver must have a valid driving licence.

Can a person without a licence join?

Yes. A child, family member or companion without a licence may sit behind a licensed driver. The price for two people sharing one quad is €35.

What is the price for one person?

One licensed driver on one quad costs €25.

Will I get dusty or wet?

Yes. The route may include dusty, muddy and wet sections, so suitable clothes and a waterproof phone case are recommended.

What should I bring?

Bring a bandana, sunglasses, sunscreen, suitable clothes and shoes, a swimsuit, towel, waterproof phone case, water and cash.

Should I book in advance?

Yes. Advance booking is recommended, especially in the high season.
