ALANYA TOUR ORGANIZATIONS — TRIP PLANNER GUIDED CONCIERGE

Flow:
1. Five seconds after a fresh Trip Planner visit, the page scrolls to Explore by Category and shows Step 1 guidance.
2. Customer selects up to 4 tours in the original category pages.
3. On the 4th selection, the site automatically returns to Trip Planner > Trip & Preferences and shows Step 2 guidance.
4. Customer enters travel dates and preferences, then presses Get recommendations.
5. Trip Planner automatically scrolls to personalized recommendations and shows Step 3 guidance.
6. Continue buttons guide the customer through analytical comparison, dates, What to Bring, and the final request step.
7. Final request goes to the travel manager for availability, pickup and final price confirmation. E-ticket/weather workflow remains the next confirmed stage after manager confirmation.

State is saved in localStorage. Intro auto-scroll is limited to once per browser session to avoid repeated interruptions.
