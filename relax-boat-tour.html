About this experience

The Relax Boat Tour offers a quieter and more intimate way to experience the Mediterranean coast of Alanya.

Unlike large party boats, this small-group cruise welcomes only 10–15 guests, creating a peaceful atmosphere without loud music, animation or crowded decks.

The route focuses on secluded bays and hidden coves that larger yachts cannot easily reach. During the day, you can swim in clear water, sunbathe on deck, relax in the shade and enjoy the sound of the sea.

This experience is ideal for couples, families and guests who prefer comfort, privacy and genuine relaxation away from busy tourist routes.
